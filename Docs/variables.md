# Area of Circular Sound Hole (A)
## sound_hole_area(r)
### Calculate the area of a circlular sound hole
- Takes one argument
    - r: Radius of the sound hole

# Speed of Sound (c)
## speed_of_sound(temp)
### Calculate the speed of sound in air
- Taks one argument
    - temp: Tempurature in degrees Celsius

# Efective Neck Length (Leff)
## round_hole(t, r)
### Calculate the effective length of a round sound hole
- Takes two arguments 
    - t: Thickness of the neck of the sound hole
    - r: Radius of the sound hole

## non_round_hole(t, a, p)
### Calculate the efective length of a non round sound hole
- Takes three arguments
    - t: Thickness of the neck of the sound hole
    - a: Area of the sound hole
    - p: Perimeter of the sound hole
